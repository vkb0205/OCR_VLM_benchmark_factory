# Vietnamese typesetting

Use only family names from the supplied font registry; every offered file has passed the Vietnamese cmap check. Preserve all source text and Unicode characters exactly. Do not transliterate or remove diacritics.

Give Vietnamese text enough line height for stacked marks. Avoid forced uppercase for prose, excessive letter spacing, and manual breaks inside words. Keep natural wrapping and disable automatic hyphenation unless a field's content explicitly contains a break. Check rendered accents on the title page, body, tables, captions, and references. Keep contrast and the 8 pt minimum after any fit adjustment.
