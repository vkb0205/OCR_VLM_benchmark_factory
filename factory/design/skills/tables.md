# Tables

Choose one valid `table_frame_type`: `full_line`, `less_line`, `fewer_line`, or `wireless_line`. Express the ruling system through that semantic frame and CSS; make headers, body rows, totals, and notes easy to distinguish without relying on color alone.

Keep the full table readable at 8 pt or larger, align numeric values consistently, and let long labels wrap. Avoid fixed row heights and clipping. Wide tables with the declared minimum column count move to the code-owned landscape table page; make that page coherent with the surrounding paper while allowing its wider measure. Do not split individual rows or add text through CSS `content`.
