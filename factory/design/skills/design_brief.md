# Design brief

Write one compact brief for this paper from its content profile and the archive summaries. State a plausible venue, era, or printing tradition and a typographic idea without borrowing a named real publication. Describe the grid by page role, the type roles and density, the color and rules, the title-block treatment, and the treatment of tables and figures.

For every neighbour supplied, write one concrete sentence explaining how this proposal differs from that design. Differences should describe choices in hierarchy, spacing, proportion, or material treatment, not vague claims of originality. Keep the brief specific enough to guide a layout plan and stylesheet, but do not invent or restate paper prose.
